# Figure 21 — The rosetta map: control ↔ mechanism ↔ law

## Purpose

The dual-framing thesis in one picture: every load-bearing mechanism has a literal control-theoretic reading and a literal constitutional-legal reading.

## Visual structure

- **Three columns**: CONTROL THEORY (ctrl palette), DEVAI MECHANISM (per-mechanism palette, bold), CONSTITUTIONAL LAW (human palette).
- **Ten rows**, each a pill-row of three boxes joined left→right by thin arrows: reference/statute book, authority-by-path/jurisdiction, effect gate/enumerated powers, severity ladder/hierarchy of norms, triage/jurisdictional assignment, RGR/petition, tie-breaker/appellate review, evidence chain/court record, dark promotion/vacatio legis, Auditor/independent judiciary.
- Footer caption: control answers dynamics questions; law answers authority questions.
