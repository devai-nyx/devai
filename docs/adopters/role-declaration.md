# Declaring a role

The normative role and authority rules live in [Articles 6–9 of the
Constitution](../../law/constitution.md). This adopter page does not restate
them. Choose the role for the session, then use the matching
[role walkthrough](../roles/) for practical guidance. A change of role requires
a session boundary.
