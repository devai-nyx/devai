## B. Amendment deltas (verified gaps — the substance W01/W04 must not lose)

### A1 — into ADR-001 (authority), sole-carried by ex-D-136
1. The zero-exemption mutator source gate: the mutator call-site denominator is mechanically derived (the predecessor's audit: 189 call sites / 49 files); zero unauthorized sites and zero exemptions; the gate demonstrably fails on stale fixtures.
2. Change control: weakening any fail-closed property requires a new explicit decision; where Articles 6–10 change, constitutional amendment. Delete/rework the predecessor's "No constitutional amendment is required" disposition line, which reads as the opposite.

### A2 — into ADR-003 (actions evidence), sole-carried by ex-D-164/D-165
1. First-parent authorization, complete: canonical record path; resolution only via `git show <base-sha>:<authorization-path>`; a record present only in the candidate head cannot authorize its own merge; missing/malformed/unresolved/revoked/nonexistent-decision → full execution.
2. Revocation triggers "malformed authorization" and "explicit revocation" (beyond audit-red, disagreement, chain failure, evidence loss); revocation restores full execution until a new complete green streak.
3. "Source PRs always run full."
4. Required-check aggregation may never translate an unjustified skip or failed freshness check into green.
5. The weekly-audit graduation conjunct runs "with promotion ignored"; thresholds inlined rather than referenced to a predecessor plan.
6. Containment: no manufactured pushes/dispatches to advance a soak or graduation window; the untouched-CI-policy list.
7. Status rewrite: mechanism accepted as law; standing = void pending re-earning per the genesis attestation.

### A3 — into ADR-004 (completion evidence), from ex-D-168
1. The universal rule: judge-only **or otherwise independently uncheckable** evidence never establishes readiness (generalize beyond the semantic-review instance).
2. The Auditor dossier's two mandatory assertions: that no readiness claim rests solely on independently uncheckable evidence, and that the campaign makes no readiness or autonomy claim at all.
3. The explicit deferral list: mutation-strength obligations and aggregation semantics are separate future decisions.
