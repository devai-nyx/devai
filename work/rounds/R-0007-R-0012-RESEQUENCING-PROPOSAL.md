# R-0007 through R-0012 resequencing record

`work/rounds/proposals/R-0007-R-0012/` is retained as non-runtime migration provenance;
canonical plans live in `work/rounds/R-0007` through `R-0012`.

1. R-0007 — CLI contract and executor substrate.
2. R-0008 — authenticated convergence-claim reuse.
3. R-0009 — product, complete user documentation, and deploy-ready site.
4. R-0010 — 1.0.0 release candidate and separately gated external publication.

## Historical migration boundary

Current `main` contains active OM-018 and exact R-0007 pre-entry/control artifacts whose
identities and internal paths bind the existing product/documentation R-0007 plan. OM-018
also directs authenticated freshness optimization and other deferred machinery into the
current R-0007 scope. Replacing only `plan.md`, `AUTHORIZATION.md`, or the orchestrator
would leave `close-control-profile.json`, `affected-test-graph.json`, claims, obligations,
closure matrices, deferrals, reviewer binding, and audit history semantically inconsistent.

OM-019 supersedes the conflicting destinations without rewriting their historical evidence.
The proposal subtree itself has no execution standing and must not be discovered as an active
round profile by runtime tooling.

## Adoption controls

OM-019 supplies the Owner rebind. The governed migration must still:

- supersedes or amends OM-018’s destination and sequencing provisions;
- binds the CLI/executor scope to the new R-0007 and the convergence-claim scope to the
  new R-0008;
- rebinds the existing product/docs and release-preparation grants to R-0009/R-0010;
- retains the external release gate and fresh R-0012 observation mandate;
- resolves the R-0007 reviewer/profile and deferred-control disposition.

The Architect performs one governed migration rather than pretending old evidence changed
identity: inventory every current R-0007 artifact and audit reference, classify it as retained,
renumbered, historical, superseded, or regenerated, update the execution contract and
governed sequencing, regenerate all candidate-bound profiles/graphs/claims/obligations and
projections, and prove there is exactly one active plan/authorization/profile per round.
No existing audit or historical evidence may be rewritten to pretend it was produced under
the new numbering.

Only canonical round paths may govern execution. The proposal subtree remains migration
history even after its useful content has been absorbed.
