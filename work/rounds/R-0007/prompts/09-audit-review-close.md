## Audit topics

- Authorization and live pre-RC standing.
- Exact action and historical counts.
- Complete 147-row migration disposition.
- Seven-domain help and hidden plumbing.
- Suite, preset, kind, slice, and tier policy/runtime/docs parity.
- Round-task containment, executor dispatch, model-routing authorization, execution
  evidence, migration refusal, and resource rollback.
- Actual capability/effect consistency.
- Output/error totality.
- User-document completeness and example validity.
- Full gates, coverage floors, generated parity, and clean state.
- Honest removed-capability and non-release disclosure.

Converge twice; the second pass is immediate, no-write, and clean. Review the exact
candidate exhaustively. Every topic receives exactly one governed disposition. Any
finding requires complete-class repair, fresh convergence, fresh audit evidence, and a
new independent review.

Close only through the then-authorized source and closure ceremony. Never infer push,
merge, publication, tagging, deployment, release, or readiness authority.
