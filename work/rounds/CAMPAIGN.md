## Dependency laws

- Rounds are serial. A downstream round may not absorb, relabel, or waive an upstream
  red.
- R-0002 and R-0003 are separate so the round that repairs operational law cannot
  certify its own founding ratification.
- BL-017 is the release throttle. No release candidate is externally publishable until
  all four unchanged coverage floors pass.
- R-0007 may prepare site bytes but may not deploy them.
- R-0008 has two phases: repository-scoped preparation is authorized; publish, tag,
  GitHub Release, and Pages deployment are pending a later Owner grant.
- R-0010 is time-dependent. Its prompt is prepared now but its authorization remains
  pending. Opening instrumentation or a zero-length streak is not completion.

## Role topology

Each role owns only its constitutional paths:

- Owner: `product/`;
- Architect: `law/`, `docs/`, `work/rounds/`, and Architect-classified root prose;
- Auditor: `work/audit/`;
- Engineer: `packages/`, workflows, scripts, and root workspace machinery;
- Inspector: `tests/` and `packages/*/tests/`;
- machine verbs only: `record/`.

The target `.devai/` boundary mandated by OM-002 is explicit. R-0002 establishes its
ignore/sentinel prerequisite and R-0005 closes the full lifecycle:

- `.devai/config/` and `.devai/pin/` are committed machine materializations of
  Architect-owned sources; the executing Engineer session runs the authorized verb and
  commits the generated bytes;
- `.devai/state/` and `.devai/worktrees/` are ignored Engineer-owned runtime state except
  for tracked `.gitkeep` sentinels.

Every role transition is a commit boundary. Cross-role features are implemented as
ordered red-contract, authority, plant, acceptance, and audit batches rather than mixed
commits.

## External side effects

OM-002 permits branches, commits, pushes, pull requests, and merges only when the exact
candidate’s required checks are green. It does not permit:

- package publication;
- tag creation or push;
- GitHub Release creation;
- GitHub Pages deployment;
- mutation of `devai-nyx/devai-original`, `../devai`, or real `stynx`;
- R-0010 streak opening or promotion activation.

The R-0008 and R-0010 authorization files contain the later human gates.
