# R-0012 — Genuine observation and promotion eligibility

## Objective

Collect real successor usage from zero, decide scorecard-skill unification from observed
evidence, and determine actions-evidence promotion eligibility without relabeling,
manufactured runs, automatic activation, or imported predecessor standing.

## Entry gates

- Fresh Owner mandate is GRANTED and binds qualification rules, observation window,
  subjects, revocation, reviewer, and later activation gate.
- R-0010 is published/closed and R-0011 is merged/closed.
- Then-current qualification/streak/source-PR/weekly/revocation/activation rules are re-read.
- Auditor independently proves zero opening standing and counters.

## Batches and observation window

| Batch | Role                     | Work                                                                            | Gate                                  |
| ----- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| B0    | Auditor                  | Establish zero baseline and operational population                              | no predecessor/synthetic run          |
| B1    | Architect                | Declare R-0012; preregister measurements and all promotion conjuncts            | no post-hoc metric choice             |
| B2    | Engineer + Inspector     | Enable only authorized observation; verify qualification and revocation drills  | instrumentation cannot change verdict |
| B3    | Auditor                  | Observe real successor runs; checkpoint source/subject/outcome/exclusions       | no duplicate/source/weekly count      |
| B4    | Architect                | Decide keep/separate or unify scorecard skills from sufficient evidence         | compatibility/migration bound         |
| B5    | Inspector                | Verify any implementation and independently recompute every graduation conjunct | all true or remain open               |
| B6    | Auditor                  | Prove zero relabeling; issue eligible or not-yet-eligible report                | chain/raw runs resolve                |
| B7    | Owner                    | Separately decide whether earned eligibility activates                          | no default activation                 |
| B8    | Architect + machine verb | Record disposition and close only when acceptance is met                        | state matches Owner act               |

The round may remain open across checkpoints. Time passing is not evidence. If the window
ends without enough qualifying observations, record not-yet-eligible; do not lower criteria.

## Acceptance, stops, and claims

Both skill decisions cite genuine usage and preserve compatibility or explicit migration.
Every promotion conjunct is independently reproducible from successor-only evidence.
Revocation restores full execution. Source PRs/weekly audits do not count. No relabeling,
duplicate, synthetic run, or predecessor standing is accepted.

Stop before B0 while authorization is pending; afterwards stop on rule change, untrusted
subject, manufactured/duplicate run, missing raw evidence, source/weekly count, relabeled
verdict, failed revocation, or inferred activation. Claim ceiling: campaign in progress;
then eligibility earned or not; activation only after B7. None implies autonomous readiness.
