## Required-control coverage

1. The three exact identities separate semantic implementation, reviewed source, and
   the mechanically bounded published head.
2. The post-PASS envelope admits only the exact Auditor review record and solely caused
   deterministic Architect projections.
3. The mutation freeze invalidates PASS after any semantic or current close-artifact
   change.
4. Candidate-only clone resolution or exact path classification establishes
   publishability without ambient-object trust.
5. Two ordered, consecutive convergence passes end with an immediate no-write/clean
   repetition.
6. Every finding triggers a complete same-class population or invariant sweep before
   another review.
7. A nonblocking finding is governed for later work or repaired before PASS, never
   repaired under a retained PASS.
8. Isolated source ancestry and closure-only PC rehearsal occurs before final review
   and creates no standing evidence.

The amended R-0006 plan makes each item an E1 failing contract, E2 semantic source, E3
workspace-tool implementation, E4 adversarial acceptance target, and E5 audit/review
gate. The orchestrator prompt blocks B0 until that exact prelude candidate passes.

## Current enforcement gap

The current sequencing policy and contract tests enforce law-before-implementation,
red-before-repair with exact semantic scope, and schema/verb ancestry before a Machine
record. The current SHA-reference check scans the decision register and Auditor
Markdown, accepting either local object resolution or exact path-scoped classification.
Those mechanisms remain valuable and unchanged by this campaign, but local object
resolution can still inherit an ambient shared object database, and neither mechanism
models the three candidate identities, two-pass convergence, review freeze/envelope,
same-class sweep, finding disposition, or isolated ceremony rehearsal.

OM-010 and the amended R-0006 E0 through E5 sequence place those missing enforcement
contracts before substantive contracts-and-coverage B0. E1 must first demonstrate the
gaps as failing Inspector contracts; E2 may define required policy/schema semantics;
E3 is limited to workspace tooling; E4 must prove acceptance and adversarial refusal;
and E5 must audit and independently challenge the exact prelude candidate. No statement
in this audit may be used as implementation or PASS evidence for E1 through E5.

challenge before B0, and the prompt enforces both the present campaign stop and the
future E5 gate. This verdict authorizes no R-0006 declaration, machinery, substantive
