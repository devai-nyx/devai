# OM-002 — Consolidated successor completion campaign

**Status:** active  
**Issued:** 2026-07-24  
**Authority:** DEVAI Owner

## Owner instruction

The Owner approved the proposed defaults with this exact instruction:

> Approve all defaults. Record OM-002. [Stop before external release / authorize
> conditional release]. [No stynx writes / authorize stynx migration].

“Approve all defaults” selects the conservative defaults that immediately preceded the
instruction:

1. repository-scoped preparation and execution are authorized, but the campaign stops
   before package publication, tag creation or push, GitHub Release creation, and GitHub
   Pages deployment;
2. the real `stynx` repository remains read-only; the executable adopter proof uses a
   hermetic in-repository stynx-shaped fixture, with an optional read-only comparison
   against the real repository;
3. a later direct Owner instruction is required to expand either boundary.

The bracketed alternatives are preserved above as provenance. They do not grant both
branches simultaneously and do not override the approved defaults.

## Purpose

Complete the DEVAI successor without laundering inherited standing: close every
confirmed gap, implement every planned feature, ratify the founding law only after an
independent preparation round, produce a release-ready 1.0.0 candidate, and open the
later evidence-re-earning campaign under fresh evidence.

The governed campaign is R-0002 through R-0010. R-0002 through R-0007 and R-0008’s
repository preparation phase may perform their repository-scoped work under this
mandate. R-0008’s external phase requires a later Owner grant. After a fully published
R-0008 close, R-0009 may perform its repository-scoped work under this mandate. R-0010
may be fully prepared now but may not open its real observation streak until a fresh
Owner mandate is recorded.

## Package and publication posture

- GitHub Packages remains the canonical, restricted `@devai-nyx/*` registry.
- The fixed 1.x group contains the ten current public successor packages plus an
  implementation-free `@devai-nyx/core` compatibility façade. The façade may stage
  canonical public assets required by the accepted packaging decisions, but it may not
  reintroduce a monolith or a dependency cycle.
- Release provenance uses GitHub artifact attestations over the exact packed tarballs,
  with hashes bound to source, release commit, package version, and published bytes.
- R-0008 may build, dry-run, attest, and prepare the release. It must stop at its
  external-release authorization gate before publishing, tagging, creating a GitHub
  Release, or deploying Pages.

## Delegation and execution

Codex may:

- prepare and execute the authorized repository-scoped rounds in serial order;
- create isolated `codex/` branches and worktrees;
- make role-pure commits, push branches, open pull requests, and merge only when every
  required local and remote gate for the exact candidate is green;
- consult Claude Fable 5 for independent planning and close review;
- dispatch bounded implementation work only when the executing environment and round
  prompt explicitly permit it, while preserving role and path ownership.

Every role change is a session or batch boundary. Commits use `DEVAI <Role>` identities.
Nothing in this delegation authorizes a role to write outside its constitutional paths
or authorizes a machine to manufacture Owner or Architect judgment.

## Claims and stop boundaries

- The frozen predecessor is read-only evidence. No fetch, checkout, configuration
  change, tag, branch, commit, release, host mutation, or working-tree write is allowed
  in `../devai` or `devai-nyx/devai-original`.
- Ratification, release, deployment, readiness, and evidence-promotion claims are
  independent. One never implies another.
- A red gate is retained as red until its acceptance contract is genuinely met.
- R-0010’s real streak and any later promotion activation require fresh, separate Owner
  instructions.
- Real `stynx` mutation and all external release actions require new authority.

## Completion condition

The work authorized without another Owner grant reaches its terminal checkpoint when
R-0002 through R-0007 have closed honestly, R-0008 has produced an independently
verified external-release candidate and handoff, R-0009/R-0010 remain fully prepared
behind their entry gates, the governed backlog has no uncatalogued deferral, and the
repository is clean.

After a later external-release grant completes R-0008, this mandate continues to
authorize R-0009’s repository-scoped preparation. Actual 1.0.0 publication and R-0010
streak opening or promotion activation are not authorized by OM-002 itself.

## Revocation

The Owner may revoke or narrow this mandate by direct instruction. Work then stops at the
nearest recoverable checkpoint; branches, worktrees, reports, and evidence are preserved
for disposition. Resumption requires a new or superseding Owner instruction.
