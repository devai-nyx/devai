# OM-014 — R-0007 through R-0009 bounded content-addressed convergence

**Status:** active

**Issued:** 2026-07-29

**Authority:** DEVAI Owner

## Prospective correction

For R-0007, R-0008, and R-0009, the Owner requires a generic per-round convergence
controller that executes only affected local tests when complete content-addressed
proof permits reuse, generates the complete semantic adversarial-review population,
and admits at most two substantive independent-review cycles for one round campaign.

This mandate is prospective. OM-012's review-until-pass continuation and OM-013's
Codex-review substitution remain immutable R-0006-only history. They neither authorize
unbounded review nor select a reviewer for R-0007 or any later round. OM-011 remains
historical evidence of the R-0006 correction; the stricter controls below govern
R-0007 through R-0009.

The pre-R-0007 machinery campaign may prepare and prove these controls, but it does not
declare or execute R-0007. Round entry remains blocked until every entry requirement,
including the separate reviewer binding required below, passes.

## Content-addressed affected-test execution

The convergence controller must use a versioned, explicit dependency DAG covering the
governed production sources, tests or stable test shards, package and cross-package
dependencies, shared helpers and setup, generators, manifests, lockfile, TypeScript and
test-runner configuration, controlling policy and schema inputs, toolchain inputs,
aggregate gates, required outputs, and whole-suite and whole-coverage fallback nodes.

Every reusable local PASS key binds the task identity, exact command and working
directory, complete known transitive input and dependency keys, required output
digests, allowlisted environment fingerprint, toolchain versions, and policy and graph
versions. Reuse is permitted only when that complete key is identical, every dependency
is fresh, every required output remains present with the same digest, and the prior
result is a structurally valid PASS. Runtime cache remains below ignored
`.devai/state/`; cache writes are atomic.

A changed test executes itself. A changed source executes every transitively dependent
test. A changed shared helper, setup file, generator, configuration, lockfile,
toolchain, environment input, policy, schema, or graph executes its complete dependent
population. Dirty, untracked, deleted, and renamed governed inputs participate in the
plan. A timestamp-only change with identical governed bytes does not invalidate a
result.

FAIL, BLOCKED, missing-output, malformed, stale, or tampered results are never reused.
An unclassified governed source, unknown dependency, ambiguous dynamic load, missing
graph edge, or incomplete population proof widens execution conservatively to the
authoritative broader population, up to the complete suite. No uncertainty may become
a skip.

Every planned task receives an auditable `EXECUTE`, `REUSE_FRESH`, or `BLOCKED`
outcome with reason codes, changed inputs, task key, dependency keys, and any fallback
population. An unchanged warm local run may hash and plan but must not start a test
process for a proven-fresh test node.

Coverage is indivisible: complete coverage may be reused only when every governed
coverage input and required stable output is byte-identical. Any relevant production,
test, provider, coverage configuration, threshold, denominator, toolchain, environment,
policy, or graph change reruns complete coverage. Partial coverage merging is
forbidden. Remote exact-head and exact-main CI begin without a trusted local cache and
execute the complete authoritative gate population.

## Semantic review obligations and structured results

Each round must maintain a schema-validated semantic obligation registry. Stable
obligations identify the claim, risk, exact governing sources and paths, required
evidence, required adversaries, freshness policy, and prior finding classes. Markdown
requirement scanning may detect unregistered prose as a lint, but it cannot create,
remove, waive, or satisfy an authoritative review topic.

The controller generates the review population from every registered obligation, the
exact base-to-candidate change population, all active controlling mandates, policies,
schemas and manifests, every current structured claim, every previous finding class in
the campaign, the current and previous candidate identities, and convergence evidence.
Every generated topic has a stable ID and exactly one structured disposition:
`RECHECKED_PASS`, `RECHECKED_FAIL`, `REUSED_FRESH_PASS`, or `BLOCKED`.

Changed and unchanged topics are equally mandatory. `REUSED_FRESH_PASS` is valid only
when the independent reviewer recomputes the current digest, inspects the evidence and
freshness proof, and records why the exact invariant still holds. A changed topic may
not use that disposition. An omitted, duplicated, unknown, incompletely evidenced, or
stale topic makes the result invalid.

Reviewer output uses schema-validated JSON or JSONL separate from any human summary.
The machine result binds the exact candidate, manifest and policy identities and ends
with an explicit terminal record whose counts match the complete parsed population.
Malformed, truncated, transport-incomplete, identity-mismatched, or count-mismatched
output is invalid and never PASS.

Each finding identifies its stable finding and defect-class IDs, severity, affected
topics, concrete evidence, deterministic population query, complete affected instance
list, and machine-checkable repair condition. Reporting one example without completing
the same-class population is `BLOCKED`, not a complete review.

Volatile current facts, including suite and topic counts, coverage, candidate ranges,
artifact hashes, gate results, and CI identities, must be generated or checked through
a structured claim ledger bound to their source commands, deterministic extractors,
candidate identity, and source digests. Candidate or source changes invalidate stale
claims.

## Candidate identity and two-cycle limit

Any semantic or current-tree repair changes the candidate identity and invalidates its
convergence result, claims, rehearsal, manifests, prior dispositions, and publication
standing. After freeze, only governed read-only review output may enter the review
envelope. PASS applies only to the exact candidate and complete manifest reviewed.

Cycle 1 is exhaustive discovery. The reviewer continues after blockers and returns all
findings and complete same-class populations in one result. A valid cycle-1 FAIL allows
one role-pure repair phase addressing every reported defect class. The campaign then
reconverges, regenerates all invalidated artifacts, freezes one new candidate, and runs
cycle 2 against the entire new topic population, not merely the repaired topics.

A valid cycle-2 FAIL or BLOCKED result transitions the campaign to
`ESCALATION_REQUIRED`. The machinery must mechanically reject cycle 3. Continued work
requires a new, separately named remediation campaign and fresh Owner authority; it may
not proceed by incrementing the review number or relabeling the same campaign.

One malformed or transport-incomplete attempt may be retried against the unchanged
candidate without consuming another substantive cycle. A second invalid transport
attempt transitions to `REVIEW_TRANSPORT_BLOCKED` and stops for escalation. Neither
transport retry nor the two-cycle budget lowers review standards or manufactures PASS.

## Model-neutral reviewer binding

R-0007, R-0008, and R-0009 each begin with an unbound reviewer slot. Before round entry,
one later active Owner mandate must bind that round to exactly one literal reviewer
model selector, an independent read-only role, the complete semantic topic census, the
two-cycle budget, and the transport-retry limit.

A missing, inactive, ambiguous, or conflicting binding blocks entry. The controller
must distinguish preparation validation from entry validation: preparation may pass
while reporting `ENTRY_BLOCKED_REVIEWER_UNBOUND`; entry validation must fail until one
exact binding resolves. Silent provider or model fallback is forbidden. A reviewer
substitution requires a new explicit Owner mandate and does not preserve a prior-model
PASS.

## Required proof

Before these controls govern a round, role-pure Inspector contracts and Auditor
evidence must prove at least:

- affected-test precision and transitive, cross-package, shared-input, and whole-suite
  invalidation;
- conservative behavior for unknown, dynamic, dirty, untracked, deleted, renamed,
  stale, failed, malformed, missing-output, and tampered states;
- atomic cache recovery, full remote execution, and whole-only coverage;
- complete DAG population and auditable impact reasons;
- semantic obligation coverage and exact-once changed and unchanged topic
  dispositions;
- rejection of stale claims, omitted topics, incomplete defect classes, truncated
  output, and wrong candidate or manifest identities;
- invalidation after repair, complete cycle-2 re-review, mechanical cycle-3 refusal,
  and transport-retry exhaustion; and
- preparation success with an unbound reviewer while round entry fails specifically
  for that unresolved binding.

## Stop and authority boundary

This mandate authorizes only preparation, implementation, review, exact-head CI,
merge, and exact-main verification of the prospective convergence machinery before
R-0007. Completion of that machinery does not declare R-0007, satisfy its reviewer
binding, or authorize B0 or later round work.

Nothing here authorizes lowered thresholds, weakened gates, manufactured PASS,
package publication, tags, GitHub Releases, Pages or other deployment, external
release, evidence promotion, real-stynx mutation, predecessor mutation, or work beyond
the separately authorized round boundary. Existing OM-002 release and deployment
prohibitions remain binding.
