# OM-019 — Adopt the six-round completion sequence

**Status:** active

**Issued:** 2026-08-05

**Authority:** DEVAI Owner

## Owner instruction

The Owner adopts this successor sequence:

1. R-0007 — CLI contract, round-subordinate tasks, and executor substrate;
2. R-0008 — authenticated convergence-claim reuse;
3. R-0009 — product semantics, complete user documentation, and deploy-ready site;
4. R-0010 — 1.0.0 release candidate, adopter proof, and separately gated external
   publication;
5. R-0011 — evidence-reuse authorization preparation; and
6. R-0012 — genuine evidence observation and promotion eligibility.

This numbering replaces the future-round destination and ordering provisions in OM-002,
OM-014, OM-017, and OM-018 where they conflict. Their safety properties, role boundaries,
coverage floors, review limits, external-effect prohibitions, and immutable historical facts
remain binding unless this rider says otherwise.

## Exact-candidate PASS recognition

The Owner recognizes the terminal Review Run 2 PASS recorded at
`work/audit/R-0007-pre-entry/om-018-review-run-2-result.md` for exact candidate
`b1a814a93b0dc186c28a1341354cdf4444609728`.

That recognition is exact-candidate evidence. It does not assert that a later commit or the

run. Review Run 3 remains forbidden. Before R-0007 B0 binds a new exact base, the Architect
must classify every commit after the recognized candidate and either prove that an existing
Owner-authorized admission rule carries the PASS across that exact delta or return the delta
to the Owner for an explicit disposition. Silence never widens the PASS.

## Scope rebinding

R-0007 now owns the pre-release CLI reduction and executor-neutral task contract. It includes
the seven workflow domains, round-subordinate task surface, `suite` / `preset` / `kind` /
`slice` / `tier` vocabulary, sense presets, check suites, `harness` spelling, `--publish`
consent, executor union, and model/runtime/effort registry described by the adopted planning
package. Full narrative user documentation belongs to R-0009; R-0007 produces only canonical
machine reference material and the minimum migration/operator contract needed to use its
surface safely.

R-0008 owns authenticated reuse of equivalent convergence sub-claims. The freshness and
affected-test work that OM-018 moved wholesale into the former R-0007 is split accordingly:
R-0007 retains the controls needed to close its own work honestly; cross-gate authentication,
signer trust, false-green adversaries, performance activation, and rollback belong to R-0008.
No whole-gate result edge among ordinary, stage2, and coverage is authorized merely by this
rebinding.

R-0009 through R-0012 receive the subjects listed above. R-0010 retains its separate external
release gate. R-0012 retains the requirement for a fresh Owner mandate before any real
observation window or promotion activity begins.

## Preparation and execution authority

The Owner authorizes the repository-scoped planning, inventory, law, red-contract,
implementation, verification, and governed close work for R-0007, subject to a valid B0
declaration and entry check under the amended execution contract.

R-0008 planning, inventory, closure-matrix completion, and entry-package preparation may run
in parallel with R-0007 planning. R-0008 implementation may not begin before R-0007 closes
and may not begin until a later Owner instruction binds its verification trust root, trust
epoch, private-key custody boundary, and independent reviewer. An unavailable or ambiguous
trust decision disables reuse; it never weakens a gate.

## Governed-plan lifecycle instruction

The Owner withdraws the earlier instruction to keep in-progress round plans uncommitted.
Canonical plans, authorizations, prompts, profiles, and other normative round inputs live
under `work/rounds/R-NNNN/` and are committed before they govern any execution. Amendments are
committed and attributable; historical intent is preserved rather than rewritten out of the
record. Close and archival material is appended only after its own evidence exists.

An uncommitted working note may assist drafting, but it has no authority, is never a candidate
input, and cannot satisfy entry, convergence, review, or close. Detached candidates, remote CI,
and third parties must be able to reproduce the complete governing input set from committed
objects alone.

## Mechanical uniqueness gate

The Owner authorizes a fail-closed repository gate that proves, for every governed round,
there is exactly one runtime-discoverable plan, authorization, close-control profile,
reviewer binding, and runtime state root when that item is required by the round state.
Proposal and working-draft roots must be explicitly non-runtime-discoverable. Duplicate,
ambiguous, missing, cross-round, or path-escaping identities fail with a specific diagnostic.

The gate is governance machinery only. It does not declare a round, grant a reviewer or
trust root, manufacture a PASS, promote a draft, or authorize implementation of R-0007 or
R-0008 scope ahead of their entry conditions.

## Unchanged prohibitions

No package publication, tag, GitHub Release, Pages deployment, evidence promotion,
real-stynx mutation, predecessor mutation, threshold reduction, population reduction,
manufactured PASS, silent fallback, or external release effect is authorized here. The
predecessor checkout remains read-only always.
