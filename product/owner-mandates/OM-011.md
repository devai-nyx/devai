# OM-011 — R-0006 content-addressed convergence and bounded review cycles

**Status:** active
**Issued:** 2026-07-28
**Authority:** DEVAI Owner

## Owner correction

The Owner interrupts R-0006 before B9 independent-review iteration 5. No further
`claude-opus-5` review and no new review candidate may begin under the current
all-or-nothing convergence machinery. R-0006 must first implement and validate the
bounded convergence-control correction in this mandate through separate role-pure
Owner, Architect, Inspector-red, Engineer, Inspector-green, and Auditor commits.

This mandate authorizes content-addressed local test-result freshness and reuse,
mandatory machine-generated adversarial-review topic coverage, and at most two
automatic review cycles for the resulting candidate. It does not weaken exact-candidate,
candidate-only clone, remote-CI, coverage, independent-review, review-freeze,
publication, or closure gates.

## Narrow supersession

OM-011 supersedes OM-010 and DII-227 only where either prohibits reuse of a local
execution result whose complete governed inputs, dependencies, outputs, toolchain,
environment, and policy version are proven identical by content address. All other
OM-010 and DII-227 requirements remain active.

This correction does not authorize evidence promotion, stale evidence, a PASS carried
across changed inputs, threshold reduction, exclusion growth, partial coverage merging,
package publication, tags, GitHub Releases, Pages deployment, external release or
deployment, real-stynx mutation, or later-round work.

## Content-addressed task freshness

Every reusable task result has a versioned task key binding:

- task ID, exact argv, and working directory;
- the test or gate file and every known transitive source input;
- dependency task keys;
- package manifests, lockfile, tsconfig, Vitest configuration, setup files, helpers,
  and generators relevant to the task;
- controlling law, policy, and schema inputs;
- toolchain versions;
- an allowlisted environment fingerprint; and
- the freshness-policy version.

Runtime cache is stored only below ignored `.devai/state/`. A task is `SKIPPED_FRESH`
only when its prior result was PASS, its task key is identical, every dependency is
fresh, required outputs still exist with identical digests, and no relevant dirty,
untracked, deleted, or renamed input or policy, toolchain, or environment change is
omitted. FAIL, malformed or tampered cache, unknown dependency, dynamic-import
ambiguity, or incomplete graph knowledge widens execution conservatively and never
produces a skip.

The machinery executes affected tests: a changed test executes itself; a changed source
executes every transitively dependent test; a changed shared helper, configuration,
lockfile, or toolchain invalidates its complete dependent population, potentially the
whole suite; and an unknown relationship runs the broader suite.

Whole coverage may be reused only when every coverage input and retained stable artifact
is identical. Any production, test, provider, coverage-configuration, threshold, or
denominator change reruns complete coverage. This correction must not implement partial
coverage merging. Remote exact-head CI begins without a trusted local cache and executes
the complete authoritative gate set.

Every convergence result classifies each task as `EXECUTED_PASS`, `SKIPPED_FRESH`,
`EXECUTED_FAIL`, or `BLOCKED`. Every skip records the task key, input digest, producing
candidate, and reused result digest.

## Complete review-scope census

The workspace tooling mechanically generates a review-scope manifest from the exact
base-to-candidate population, controlling law and policy, every R-0006 requirement,
every finding and defect class from reviews 1 through 4, and the current and previous
candidate manifests. Every mandatory topic contains its topic ID, claim, governing
paths, current and previous digests, changed status, required adversaries, previous
findings, freshness proof, and required disposition.

A review record is structurally invalid unless it contains exactly one disposition for
every topic: `RECHECKED_PASS`, `RECHECKED_FAIL`, `REUSED_FRESH_PASS`, or `BLOCKED`.
Unchanged status never permits omission. For a reused fresh pass, the reviewer must
independently recompute the digest, inspect the freshness proof, and state why the
invariant still holds for the current candidate. Previous finding classes remain
mandatory after repair.

## Review-cycle budget

Cycle 1 is exhaustive discovery: the reviewer continues after a blocker and returns all
findings and complete same-class populations in one response. Repairs address the full
defect classes in role-pure batches. Cycle 2 is one complete re-review. If cycle 2
fails, execution stops and escalates to the Owner with a process and root-cause report;
review 3 may not begin silently. The two-cycle limit is a retry budget and never forces
or manufactures PASS.

## Required adversarial proof

Inspector contracts must prove unchanged-task reuse; dependent-test invalidation for a
changed source; complete invalidation for shared helper, configuration, lockfile,
toolchain, environment, and policy changes; dirty, untracked, deleted, and renamed input
invalidation; failed, stale, malformed, and tampered cache rejection; conservative
unknown and dynamic-dependency behavior; complete coverage rerun after any governed
coverage-input change; exact-once review-topic coverage; rejection of omitted,
duplicated, or unverified unchanged topics; and retention of every previous finding
class.

## Stop and exit boundary

Do not begin independent-review iteration 5 until OM-011, its Architect decision and
policy/schema definitions, Inspector red and green contracts, Engineer machinery,
Auditor audit, smart convergence, and complete review-scope manifest are committed and
green. This mandate authorizes preparation of a ready-to-review SHA only. It does not
itself authorize treating that SHA as reviewed, passing, published, merged, closed, or
released.
