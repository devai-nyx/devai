# OM-001 — Production-grade DEVAI completion under Codex steering

**Status:** superseded — attested-historical; not an active successor mandate
**Issued:** 2026-07-15  
**Authority:** DEVAI Owner (Constitution Article 6)

## Purpose

Leave DEVAI production-grade, self-consistent, and complete: every supported policy and product claim must have implemented, tested, self-adopted machinery, with recurring governance and shipping failures structurally prevented.

## Delegation

- Codex steers the program on the Owner's behalf and makes final implementation and sequencing decisions within this mandate.
- Codex may exchange messages with Claude Fable 5 as often as needed to discuss architecture, plans, techniques, decisions, execution evidence, and independent verification.
- After joint plan refinement, Codex may dispatch and execute the agreed rounds through role-declared, isolated worktrees and the repository's required gates.
- This delegation remains active until the completion conditions are proven or the Owner revokes or supersedes it.

## Required outcomes

1. Close every confirmed audit gap rather than relabeling or silently deferring it.
2. Implement constitutionally narrow, fail-closed role and resource enforcement across every supported mutation boundary.
3. Split the skills monolith into coherent modules without weakening behavior, evidence, lifecycle, or consent contracts.
4. Make documentation, public artifacts, release state, scorecards, and closure attestations current and mutually consistent.
5. Exercise DEVAI on itself and retain independently verifiable evidence for every production-readiness claim.

## Work sharing

- Codex owns the most critical and complex authority-enforcement round and final integration decisions.
- Claude owns a distinct, non-overlapping round, including the skills-monolith decomposition, after joint architectural agreement.
- Codex dispatches Sol and Claude dispatches Fable. Each orchestrator may size and dispatch bounded workers.
- Parallel work uses distinct Git worktrees with explicit file ownership and declared role boundaries.
- After each round, Sol asks Fable and Fable asks Sol for an independent assessment. Unresolved findings block close.

## Gates and finish condition

Close requires build, lint, format check, typecheck, unit, integration, regression, contract, smoke, supported E2E, experimental-containment, merged coverage at 70/60/70/70 or higher, schema/invariant/journey/trace/test-trace validation, generated-reference identity, documentation links, evidence-chain verification, governance checks, and a clean repository. Independent cross-model review and a final requirement-by-requirement production-readiness audit must prove the claims against current source, runtime behavior, published artifacts, and remote release state.

## Boundaries

- This mandate does not authorize either model to change the Owner's product intent, erase uncertainty, weaken gates, expose secrets, or claim control outside a verified host boundary.
- Autonomous-loop machinery remains experimental unless a later Owner-authorized constitutional promotion proves otherwise.
- Role transitions require explicit session or batch boundaries and remain attributable in the round record.
- This mandate does not authorize communication with parties other than Claude, repository-scoped GitHub surfaces, and explicitly dispatched worker agents.

## Revocation

The Owner may revoke or narrow this delegation at any time by direct instruction to Codex or Claude; no repository edit is required for revocation to take effect. The recipient must notify the collaborating model and all active orchestrators before any new dispatch, mutation, merge, publication, or external communication. Active work stops at the nearest recoverable checkpoint; branches, worktrees, logs, and evidence are preserved for Owner disposition. Work resumes only after a new or superseding Owner mandate is acknowledged and its scope is recorded.
