# OM-003 — Claude review model narrowing

**Status:** active
**Issued:** 2026-07-24
**Authority:** DEVAI Owner

## Owner instruction

The Owner narrowed the Claude-review delegation with this exact instruction:

> suspend Claude interaction till its quota is back. After that, when returning, use
> only OPUS 5 instead of Fable 5.

## Applied boundary

No Claude interaction may occur while the current quota is unavailable. After the quota
recovers, every new Claude planning, challenge, or close-review interaction for this
campaign must use Claude Opus 5 through the explicit CLI model selector
`claude-opus-5`. Fable 5 is no longer an authorized selector for new interactions.

Historical Fable reviews remain immutable evidence of what was actually reviewed. Their
model attribution is not rewritten, upgraded, or treated as an Opus review.

This rider narrows only OM-002’s Claude model selection. Every other OM-002
authorization, human gate, role boundary, claims ceiling, predecessor prohibition,
real-stynx prohibition, and external-release boundary remains unchanged.

## Resume condition

The first post-recovery Claude call must:

1. use `claude-opus-5` explicitly;
2. identify the exact candidate commit being reviewed;
3. remain read-only; and
4. preserve any quota or model-selection failure as a blocker rather than falling back
   to Fable.
