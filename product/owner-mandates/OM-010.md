# OM-010 — Pre-R-0006 governance alignment and entry-control prelude

**Status:** active
**Issued:** 2026-07-27
**Authority:** DEVAI Owner

## Owner instruction

The Owner authorizes one bounded governance-alignment campaign before R-0006. This
campaign may amend the shared execution contract and the still-dormant R-0006 plan and
orchestrator prompt, record an Auditor preflight alignment report, and merge those
control-only changes after exact local and remote validation.

The campaign must stop after its merge and exact-main CI. It does not declare or execute
R-0006, implement plant or test machinery, append an R-0006 decision, or begin any
R-0006 batch.

## R-0006 entry-control grant

After the alignment campaign is merged and independently revalidated at live entry,
R-0006 may execute only the mandatory E0 through E5 entry-control prelude before its
existing contracts-and-coverage work:

1. Architect declaration binding the amended control contract and the exact plan and
   prompt digests;
2. Inspector failing contracts for the admitted control defects;
3. Architect policy and schema semantics required by those contracts;
4. Engineer workspace-tooling implementation only;
5. Inspector acceptance and adversarial verification; and
6. Auditor audit plus independent read-only challenge of the exact prelude candidate.

The existing R-0006 B0 through B9 work remains blocked until E5 records PASS on an
exact, clean, publishable candidate. This grant does not predetermine that PASS and does
not claim that any entry-control machinery exists before E1 through E5 implement and
verify it.

## Required control outcomes

The prelude may mechanically enforce candidate identity, candidate-only clean-clone
publishability, two-pass convergence, the post-review mutation freeze and bounded
review envelope, same-class correction sweeps, and isolated source/closure ceremony
rehearsal. It may change only workspace tooling and Inspector contracts under the
role-pure E0 through E5 sequence; it may not begin substantive R-0006 action-contract,
coverage, plant, or test-depth work.

## Boundaries and nonclaims

This mandate does not authorize threshold or exclusion changes, evidence reuse or
promotion, package publication, tags, GitHub Releases, Pages deployment, external
release or deployment, real-stynx mutation, or R-0007 and later work. It does not alter
OM-003's Claude selector boundary or manufacture any Claude, Opus, independent-review,
or cross-provider PASS.

The predecessor remains read-only. Closed R-0001 through R-0005 plans, prompts, audits,
decisions, and PC records remain immutable. Any scope drift, red gate, role impurity,
unpublishable identity, or nonconvergent candidate stops execution.
