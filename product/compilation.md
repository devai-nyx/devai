# Compilation (Article 12)

Product artifacts reference the invariants they depend on via `related_invariants`;
the link is explicit and one-directional (Owner intent compiles down to Architect
law). REV-0006 finding carried to W01: no invariant currently anchors back to a
product artifact, so the cross-tier guard has an empty domain — W01 decides whether
originating invariants carry product anchors (making the guard real) or the guard
is recorded dormant-until-first-anchor.
